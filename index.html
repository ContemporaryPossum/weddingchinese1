Our story

Nine years, one small umbrella and a great deal of white coffee.

  1. 2017

    Caught in the rain

    A monsoon downpour in old Kuala Lumpur pushed two strangers under the same five-foot way. One small umbrella, a lot of laughing, and neither of us was in a hurry for the rain to stop.

  2. 2018

    White coffee for two

    Our first proper date: kaya toast, half-boiled eggs and three refills at a corner kopitiam. He insisted on paying. She insisted on next time.

  3. 2021

    Sunsets in Penang

    After a long year apart, we met again on a quiet jetty in George Town and decided the distance was over.

  4. 2025

    Yes, in the tea fields

    Sunrise in Cameron Highlands. Through the mist, he knelt with a ring he had hidden for months. She said yes before he finished the question.

  5. 2026

    Two families, one table

    At our guo da li, baskets of oranges and dragon-phoenix bangles changed hands, our parents gave their blessing, and the countdown began.
